WHAT WE DID, IN ONE PARAGRAPH
-------------------------------
Kalpit is building software that reads Hindi land-sale documents and turns
them into text automatically (this is called OCR). Your job is to test how
well that reading works, not to build it. We installed a free, well-known
OCR tool called Tesseract, ran it on your 3 real documents, then deliberately
made worse (blurry/tilted/compressed) copies and ran it again to see how
much it struggled. We wrote Python code that automatically compares what
Tesseract read against the correct answer and calculates error scores.
Finally, we wrote up the results and answered the company's 5 strategy
questions about testing this kind of system in the real world.

If anything above is still confusing, that's completely fine -- just ask,
and we'll go through it slowly, one piece at a time.
